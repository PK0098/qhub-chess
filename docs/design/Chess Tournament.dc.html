§ 01 / How it works

The rules,
briefly.

Five rules. That's it. Read them once, then go find someone to play.

Need a clock?

Grab the free Chess.com clock app. Set it to 10 + 0.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play
  1. 1.♜
    One game each
    Every pair of players plays each other once — a single round robin. Colors are decided by coin toss (or rock-paper-scissors, we're not picky).
  2. 2.♛
    Ten minutes each
    Every game is played with a 10-minute timer per player (10+0, no increment).
  3. 3.♚
    3 · 1 · 0
    Win = 3 points, Draw = 1 point, Loss = 0 points.
  4. 4.♝
    Play nice
    Standard chess rules apply. Touch-move, be nice, have fun.
  5. 5.♞
    Your game, your schedule
    Once pairings are out you get an email with your opponent's address and a report link. Reply to agree a time and a corner of Anahit, play, then report the result. If you already know each other, skip the emails and just play.
  6. 6. (unofficial)
    Losing to the intern is not a bug.
    It's a feature.
